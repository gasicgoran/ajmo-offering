A good start online, and someone to look after it

A logo is a small thing that carries a lot. It goes on your door, your documents, your website and your invoices. Done well, you will still be using it in twenty years, and your clients will recognise you by it. It is worth taking the time to get it right.

We can start with just that. Or we can go further — website, social media, content, and keeping everything current. You decide how far to go, and you can always add more later.

What you can choose

Seven areas, each with a few options. They differ in scope, not in care — we finish whichever one you choose properly. Most companies mix them: a simple option in one area, a fuller one in another.

Logo and brand materials

Website

Start with what you need now. A site can grow later, and in most cases without being rebuilt.

Essentials website

A complete, compact site covering what you do, who you do it for, a few words about your team, and how to reach you — along with the legal and privacy pages. Everything sits on one clearly structured page, which works particularly well on a phone and is quick to put live.

Small site, 4–6 pages

Separate pages for your services, about you, contact and the legal information. Room to say more, without much to maintain.

Larger site, 7–10 pages

A page for each service, a team page, a careers page if you are hiring, and a news section.

Languages

Worth deciding early, because it affects the website and everything published later.

One language (German)

Simplest to keep current.

Machine translation, reviewed if needed

We translate and tidy it up. Quick and affordable, and a good fit for a second language you mainly need for information.

Professional translation

A translator does the work and your colleague reads it through, so the terms sound right for your field. We organise the translator and coordinate the whole thing if you prefer.

Social media profiles and the newsletter are in German. That reaches the people nearby — families in your area and anyone thinking of working with you.

Photographs

Send us whatever photographs you already have and we'll use the ones that work. For everything else, there are three ways to go.

Free library images

We choose them, check that they can be used commercially, and prepare them for the site. A practical and common choice.

Paid images with licences

If you'd like a more specific look, we find and purchase the images and keep the licences on file for you.

Photoshoot

If at some point you'd like photographs of your own team and premises, we can organise a photographer, prepare a list of shots and handle the consent forms. This can be done once and refreshed every couple of years.

Social media

We work with Instagram and Facebook, and with an email newsletter, all in German. Choose the level of activity that suits you — a quiet, well-kept profile is a perfectly good choice.

Profiles only

We set up Instagram and Facebook so they match your brand: profile photo, cover, description, contact details, a link to your site.

Holidays and key dates

Planned once for the year ahead and then it simply runs. The Swiss public holidays worth marking — New Year, Easter, 1 August, Christmas — your opening hours around them, your company anniversary, and one or two events of your own. We prepare the calendar, you approve it once, and the posts go out on the dates.

Regular posting

A few posts a week on Instagram and Facebook. We prepare the text and the visuals; you approve them. Typical posts:

  • Who we are, and what we do
  • Our services, one at a time
  • Our team
  • Where to find us and when we are open
  • What makes us different, and why people choose us
  • Useful information about care, explained simply
  • Holiday and seasonal greetings

Two practical points

  • Where the ideas come from. At the start of each month we put the plan together in a short call — you tell us what is happening, we suggest the rest.
  • Replies to messages and comments. These stay with you, since they often need someone who knows the client and the situation. If you would prefer us to handle them, we can look at it separately and agree how it would work.

Content

Whatever we agree, some of the material has to come from you — you know your work and your clients. The difference between these options is how much of the rest we take on.

You provide the content

You send us the text and the ideas, we design the visuals and send them back, and you publish.

You give us the topic

You tell us the subject, we write it and design it, you review it, and you publish.

We plan it together

At the start of the month we agree a rough plan, we ask you for any details we need, then we prepare and design everything, send it for your approval and publish it.

Newsletter

An email in German, once a month or once a quarter, to people who have asked to hear from you. Same principle: you approve it, we send it.

Looking after the website

Someone needs to keep the site running and up to date. You can do it yourself — we'll show you how and leave written instructions — or we can take it on.

Basic

We keep the website's software current, make regular copies of the site so nothing gets lost, and check that it is online and working. If something goes wrong, we deal with it.

Standard

Everything above, plus small changes whenever you need them: new text, a new photograph, a changed price, a job posting. Just send us an email.

Full

Everything above, plus we go through the site a few times a year, refresh text and photographs that have aged, and let you know what is worth updating.

What is not included in these prices

Some things are paid directly to the providers rather than to us, because they are ongoing subscriptions in your name. We set them all up and manage them for you, we never add a margin to them, and you keep ownership of everything. These are Swiss providers and they bill in francs.

  • Domain registration, renewed annually
  • Website hosting
  • SSL security certificate
  • Business email at your own domain
  • Image licences, if you choose paid photographs
  • Translation, where a professional translator is involved
  • A photographer, if you decide on a photoshoot

Translation and photography are one-off costs rather than subscriptions, and we will give you exact figures before anything is ordered.
